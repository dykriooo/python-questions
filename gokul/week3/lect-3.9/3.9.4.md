str.format()
Example: print("Hello, {}. You are {} years old.".format(name, age))

the % Operator (Old Style Formatting)
Example: print("Hello, %s. You are %d years old." % (name, age))

Template Strings (string.Template)
from string import Template
t = Template("Hello, $name. You are $age years old.")
print(t.substitute(name=name, age=age))
